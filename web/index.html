Evidence, Assumptions & Limitations

What this shows: diffusion of released free drug outward from the nanoparticle through a homogeneous generic soft-tissue medium, coloured directly from the finite-difference solution of ∂C/∂t = D∇²C − kₑC (spherical symmetry). The play head, heatmap and depth graph all read the same solver snapshots.

What this does NOT show: nanoparticle carriers moving through tissue, crossing membranes, entering cells, or accumulating in a named organ; whole-body biodistribution; barrier/partition effects.

  • Evidence grade: ILLUSTRATIVE_ONLY — teaching/qualitative use.
  • Model status: code-verified & numerically-verified; not calibrated or externally validated.
  • Parameters: the tissue diffusivity, clearance and geometry are the on-screen slider values — order-of-magnitude illustrative, not literature-qualified for any specific tissue.
  • Penetration depth = deepest point where C exceeds 10% of the surface concentration (threshold shown; the definition is fixed here and will be user-adjustable in named-tissue profiles).
  • Not a clinical decision tool; does not predict any individual’s response.
