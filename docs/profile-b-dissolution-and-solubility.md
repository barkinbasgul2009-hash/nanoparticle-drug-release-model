# Profile B — Celastrol Dissolution and Solubility

Machine-readable: `data/profile-b-solubility-registry.json`.

## Qualitative consensus (recorded) vs numbers (not recorded)
Celastrol (tripterine) is, by consensus across reviews (Front Pharmacol 2020;11:558741
PMC7751759; Front Pharmacol 2024 PMC10904542):
- **poorly water-soluble**,
- **chemically unstable** under aqueous / physiological / oxidative conditions,
- a **quinone-methide Michael acceptor** that reacts with thiols.

**Numeric values are deliberately NOT recorded.** Web search returned candidate figures
(an aqueous-solubility number, a β-cyclodextrin ~30-fold enhancement, a 96-h degradation
percentage) but **no primary/full text was opened**, so recording them would breach the
no-numbers-from-search rule. Required primaries: equilibrium aqueous solubility vs pH at
37 °C; logP/logD; degradation kinetics (t90); dissolution/supersaturation behaviour.

## Modelability
Released-celastrol dissolution/precipitation is at most a **qualitative risk layer (S1)**.
A quantitative supersaturation-precipitation model is **not** supported.
